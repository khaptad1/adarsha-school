Est. 2034 BS

Adarsha Sanothimi H.S. School

Adarsha Sanothimi Higher Secondary School is a government-aided school located in Sanothimi, Bhaktapur. Serving the community since 2034 BS, we provide education from ECD through Grade 12.

We offer +2 programs in Science, Management and Humanities under NEB. Our school is known for its dedicated teachers, disciplined environment and strong exam results.

Frequently Asked Questions

For admission you need: Birth certificate, Previous school's Transfer Certificate (TC), Mark sheet/Report card of last class, 4 passport-size photos, Citizenship copy of parents, and Migration certificate if from another district.
We offer three streams under NEB: Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Mathematics), Management (Accountancy, Economics, Business Studies), and Humanities (Social Studies, Literature, Sociology/Psychology). Admission is based on SEE GPA and entrance exam.
Yes. Students with SEE GPA of 3.9 or above may qualify for fee concessions. Economically disadvantaged students can apply for government scholarship. Contact the school office for details.
School runs from Sunday to Friday. Morning session: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM (winter: 10:00 AM – 3:30 PM). ECD students: 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM. Office timings: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM.
Parents can visit the school office to get attendance records. For regular updates, parent-teacher meetings are held every trimester. You may also contact the class teacher directly.
